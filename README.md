# Song for Song

A music battle tournament. Players link Spotify or Apple Music, bring a crate of songs, and battle song for song. Each match is best two out of three. Win and you advance through the bracket until one champion is left.

## Try it

Open `index.html` in any web browser (double-click it). Nothing to install.

## How it works

1. **Players**: 8 players, each with a name, a music service, and 3 songs.
2. **Bracket**: players are shuffled into quarterfinals, semifinals, and a final.
3. **Battle**: each round both players pick a song. Tap the winning song, or let the crowd vote.
   First to 2 round wins advances.
4. **Champion**: the last player standing wins.

## Status

This is a clickable prototype. Songs are sample data and the music service is a label.
Next steps for a real app: sign in with Spotify / Apple Music, load real playlists,
play song previews, and let people vote from their own phones.
