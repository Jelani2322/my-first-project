# Song for Song

A music battle tournament. Players link Spotify or Apple Music and battle with songs from their own
Liked Songs, library, and playlists. Each round has a topic, and you have to pick a song that fits it.
The crowd listens to both songs and votes. Best two out of three wins, and winners advance through
the bracket until one champion is left.

## Try it

Open `index.html` in a web browser. With no keys set it runs in demo mode with sample songs.

To put it online and connect real accounts, follow **[SETUP.md](SETUP.md)**.

## How a tournament works

1. **Players:** pick 2, 4, or 8 players. Each one links Spotify or Apple Music.
2. **Genre:** pick the genre for the tournament (or any genre).
3. **Bracket:** players are shuffled into matchups.
4. **Battle:** each round shows a topic. Both players pick a song from their own music, the crowd
   hears a 30-second preview of each, and everyone votes. First to 2 round wins advances.
5. **Champion:** the last player standing wins.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole app
- `config.js`: your Spotify and Apple Music keys
- `SETUP.md`: step-by-step setup
